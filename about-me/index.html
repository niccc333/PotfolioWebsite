Samantha Graham

Sam is a Makeup Artist and creative based in Tiohtià:ke/Montréal, studying Art History and Gender, Sexuality and Feminism, which is at the core of her work. Her approach is to use makeup as a mode of character building. With a focus on fashion and editorial, she is expanding her work outside of makeup and into directing projects of her own. Outside of work, she is a lover and collector of vintage fashion and makeup.
